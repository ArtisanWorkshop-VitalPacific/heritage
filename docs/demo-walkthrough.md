# Walkthrough for Susan

About 20 minutes. Best on a laptop in Chrome or an iPad in Safari, with sound on. Allow the microphone when asked.

Live: https://heritage-readers.vercel.app

## Presenter tools

- **Walkthrough.** The button at the foot of any grown-up page, or press `?` on a keyboard. Nine stops. Each one sets the role and the child, then opens the page.
- **View as.** Top right of the app: Parent, Teacher, Admin, Student, and More (Owner, Aide, Observer).
- **Reset demo.** Foot of any grown-up page. Restores the starting families. Everything lives in this browser, so another device starts fresh.

## 1. The website (3 minutes)

1. Open the home page and scroll slowly. The engraving film plays with the scroll: the boy reads, the page turns, the frame opens. Then "Now the page listens."
2. Keep scrolling: the lesson in three parts, the Hay & Wingo vowel ladder (tap a word to hear it, then "Climb the ladder"), the reading check, the fables, every lesson prints, the parent report, roles, Susan, pricing.
3. Top bar: Method (the 40 rungs, mastery, what the AI decides), Schools, Pricing, Susan.

## 2. A new family (4 minutes)

1. Open the app and choose "Add a child", or Walkthrough stop 2.
2. Family first, then the child: name, age (the age band appears), interests, narrator voice ("Hear it"), faith track, screen limit, paper or screen, consent.
3. "Start placement" hands the device to the child.

## 3. Placement (3 minutes)

Straight on from the new family. Letter sounds (the grown-up taps), then words (the child reads aloud and the microphone listens), then a short passage. The result places the child on a rung and opens the first lesson. Placement belongs to new children: Nora and Eli are already placed.

## 4. A lesson as Nora (5 minutes)

Nora is 6, on rung 10 (the letter h), with one clean read already.

1. Walkthrough stop 3, or "Hand the device to Nora" on the family home.
2. Warm up, the letter h, the vowel ladder, word pairs, then the story "The Hen in the Hat."
3. "Begin reading" and read aloud. The underline follows the reader. A read at 95% or better is her second clean read: rung 10 is mastered and the seal appears.
4. Talk with Mrs. Wren: hold the microphone and ask "What does diligent mean?", "Why do I need to read?", "Tell me a fable" or "How do I say hat?"
5. "Grown-ups" (top right), then "Return."

## 5. The parent's proof (3 minutes)

1. **Report.** Rungs mastered, instructional level, words correct per minute against the grade norm, the week 1 and week 8 recordings (sample audio), the next three skills, minutes on screen next to minutes on paper.
2. **Print.** This week's packets. Open one: the teacher's script, the student page, and the score sheet with its QR code.
3. **Give a month.** Copy the code, invite an email, mark it paid (a demo control), and both families are credited.

## 6. Roles (4 minutes)

1. **Teacher** (Eleanor Finch, Primer Class): the mastery board, a paper score, class packets by rung.
2. **Admin** (Ruth Adler): members, change a role, the roles and permissions matrix, the audit log, privacy and export.
3. As Teacher, open Organization. The role gate says who may open it.
4. **Student:** only the lesson. No settings, no sharing.
