# Walkthrough for Susan

About 20 minutes. Best on a laptop in Chrome or an iPad in Safari, with sound on. Allow the microphone when asked.

Live: https://heritage-readers.vercel.app

**Before you start: press Reset demo** (foot of any grown-up page). A rehearsal moves Nora past rung 10, and the mastery moment needs her on rung 10 with one clean read.

## Presenter tools

- **Walkthrough.** The button at the foot of any grown-up page, or press `?` on a keyboard (on the website too). Ten stops. Each one sets the role and the child, then opens the page.
- **View as.** Top right of the app: Parent, Teacher, Admin, Student, and More (Owner, Aide, Observer).
- **Reset demo.** Foot of any grown-up page. Restores the starting families. Everything lives in this browser, so another device starts fresh.

## 1. The website (3 minutes)

1. Open the home page and scroll slowly. The engraving film plays with the scroll: the boy reads and turns the page while the frame opens around him and the mother. The closing line is "Then your child reads the story to Mrs. Wren."
2. Keep scrolling: the lesson in three parts, the Hay & Wingo vowel ladder (tap a word to hear it, then "Climb the ladder"), the reading check, the fables, every lesson prints, the parent report, roles, Susan, pricing.
3. Top bar: Method (the 40 rungs, mastery, what the AI decides), Schools, Pricing, Our Story (Susan).

## 2. A new family (4 minutes)

1. Open the app and choose "Add a child", or Walkthrough stop 2.
2. Family first, then the child: name, age (the age band appears), interests, narrator voice ("Hear it"), faith track, screen limit, paper or screen, consent.
3. "Start placement" hands the device to the child.

## 3. Placement (3 minutes)

Straight on from the new family. Letter sounds (the grown-up taps), then words (the child reads aloud and the microphone listens), then a short passage. The result places the child on a rung and opens the first lesson. Placement belongs to new children: Nora and Eli are already placed.

## 4. A lesson as Nora (5 minutes)

Nora is 6, on rung 10 (the letter h), with one clean read already.

1. Walkthrough stop 3, or "Hand the device to Nora" on the family home.
2. Warm up, the letter h, the vowel ladder, word pairs, then the story "The Hen in the Hat."
3. "Begin reading" and read aloud. The underline follows the reader. A read at 95% or better is her second clean read: rung 10 is mastered and the seal appears. When a grown-up reads, the rate is faster than any six-year-old, so it is marked "Not timed" and her fluency chart stays true. Worth pointing out.
4. Talk with Mrs. Wren: hold the microphone and ask "What does diligent mean?", "Why do I need to read?", "Tell me a fable" or "How do I say hat?"
5. "Grown-ups" (top right), then "Return."

## 5. The parent's proof (3 minutes)

1. **Report.** Rungs mastered, instructional level, words correct per minute against the grade norm, the first and latest recordings (sample audio), the next three skills, minutes on screen next to minutes on paper.
2. **Print.** This week's packets. Open one: the teacher's script, the student page, and the score sheet with its QR code.
3. **Give a month.** Copy the code, invite an email, mark it paid (a demo control), and both families are credited.

## 6. Roles (4 minutes)

1. **Teacher** (Eleanor Finch, Primer Class): the mastery board, a paper score, class packets by rung.
2. **Admin** (Ruth Adler): members, change a role, the roles and permissions matrix, the audit log, privacy and export.
3. Walkthrough stop 9, "A role gate": the teacher opens Organization and the gate says who may open it.
4. **Student:** only the lesson. No settings, no sharing.

## Notes

- Mrs. Wren answers from a script tonight. Add a payment card to the Vercel account and the AI Gateway turns on by itself: live replies and generated passages, still checked word by word by the validator.
- Her voice is ElevenLabs (Eleven v4 Turbo for her lines, Multilingual v2 for single words). The family's narrator setting picks the voice: Gentle Mara (American) or Sandy Soft (British). If ElevenLabs can't answer, the browser voice takes over.
- Tap a lesson word printed with a pink vowel (the ladder, the warm up, the drills, a word in the story) and she holds the vowel, then says the word: "top" is "ta, aah, op... top." Eleven v4 reads the sounds from IPA. Each word is made once per voice and kept, so the first tap of a new word takes about two seconds and every tap after plays at once. Heart words (the, said, was) are said plainly.
- Speech is scored by the browser's recognizer. Production uses a child-tuned scorer and Susan's recorded phonograms.
- Ladder colors were sampled from photographs of Reading with Phonics. McGuffey colors are provisional until scans arrive.
- "Mrs. Wren" is a working name.
