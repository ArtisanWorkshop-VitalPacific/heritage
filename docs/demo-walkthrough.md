# Walkthrough for Susan

About 20 minutes. Best on an iPad in Safari or a laptop in Chrome, with sound on. Allow the microphone when asked.

Demo mode: everything is stored in this browser. "Reset demo" at the bottom of any grown-up screen restores the starting data.

## 1. The site (3 minutes)

1. Open the home page. Scroll slowly through the hero: the engraving film plays with the scroll, and the boy turns the page.
2. Keep scrolling: the lesson in 15 minutes, then the Hay & Wingo ladder. Tap a word on the right ladder to hear it. Press "Climb the ladder."
3. Point out the reading check, the Aesop engravings, paper first, and the parent proof.
4. Open Method, Schools, Pricing and Susan from the top bar.

## 2. A new family (4 minutes)

1. Press "Begin placement" (or open the app and choose "Add a child").
2. Create a family, then a child: name, age (the age band appears), interests, narrator voice ("Hear it"), faith track, screen limit, paper or screen, consent.
3. Start the placement: letter sounds (the grown-up taps), then words (the child reads; the mic listens), then a short passage.
4. The result places the child on a rung and starts the first lesson.

## 3. A lesson as Nora (5 minutes)

Use the seeded Harper family: Nora is 6, on rung 10, the letter h, with one clean read already.

1. From the family home press "Hand the device to Nora."
2. Begin today's lesson: warm-up words, the letter page for h, the vowel ladder, the word pairs, then the passage "The Hen in the Hat."
3. Press "Begin reading" and read the passage aloud. The underline follows the reader.
4. A clean read at 95% or better is her second clean read: the rung is mastered and the seal appears.
5. Talk with Mrs. Wren: hold the mic and ask "What does honest mean?" or "Why do I need to read?"
6. "Grown-ups" (top right) returns to the parent view.

## 4. The parent's proof (3 minutes)

1. Report: rungs mastered, instructional level, words correct per minute against the grade norm, the week 1 and week 8 recordings (sample audio in the demo), next three skills, minutes on screen next to minutes on paper.
2. Print: open this week's lesson packet. Teacher script, student page, score sheet with the lesson code.
3. Give a month: the referral code, invite an email, mark it paid (demo), and see both families credited.

## 5. Roles (4 minutes)

Use "View as" at the top right.

1. Teacher (Mrs. Finch, Oak Hill Primer Class): the mastery board, a student's detail, enter a paper score, class packets by rung.
2. Admin (Ruth Adler): members, change a role, the roles and permissions matrix, the audit log, privacy.
3. Switch back to Teacher and try to open Organization: the role gate explains who may open it.
4. Student: only the lesson. No settings, no sharing.

## Notes

- The tutor's voice is the browser's voice unless ElevenLabs is configured (one environment variable).
- Speech scoring uses the browser's recognizer. Production uses a child-tuned scorer and Susan's recorded phonograms.
- The ink colors for the ladder were sampled from photographs of Reading with Phonics; McGuffey colors are provisional until scans arrive.
